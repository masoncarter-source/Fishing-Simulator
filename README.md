# Stillwater

A first-person fishing simulator on a Yellowstone-inspired alpine lake. The scene uses photorealistic artwork with an animated rod, bobber, line, and ripples.

Download or clone the repository and open `index.html` in a browser. All assets are included and the game runs offline.

- Click or tap the lake to aim, or press **Space** to cast straight ahead.
- When the bobber dips and **Fish on** appears, press **Space** or tap **Set hook**.
- Hold **Space** or **Hold to reel**. Ease off when line tension rises, then resume reeling once the fish slows down.
- Open **Catch log** to see your fish and personal bests. The remade game starts a fresh log and saves it in the current browser when storage is available.
